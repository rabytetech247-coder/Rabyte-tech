Seller Policy

Guidelines for listing your products on our platform.

1. Accurate Representation

All product images, descriptions, and features must accurately represent the final product the buyer receives.

2. Prohibited Content

We do not allow the sale of malicious software, plagiarized content, or assets that violate third-party copyright (DMCA).

3. Support Expectations

Sellers are expected to provide reasonable support for their digital products. Failure to support buyers may result in account suspension and trust score penalties.
