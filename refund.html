Refund Policy

Understanding refunds on a decentralized marketplace.

Our Role in Transactions

Rabyte-Tech is a zero-fee discovery platform for digital products. When you click "Buy" on a product, you are securely redirected to the creator's preferred checkout platform (such as Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, Stripe, or Payhip). We do not process your credit card or hold your funds at any time.

How to Request a Refund

Because the transaction occurs directly between you and the creator on their payment processor, all refund requests must be directed to the creator. You can usually find the creator's contact information on their checkout page or within your email receipt.

Platform-Specific Refund Policies

  • Gumroad: Generally offers a 30-day refund window if the creator has enabled it, or you can contact the creator directly.
  • Lemon Squeezy: Contact the seller directly through your purchase receipt.
  • Stripe: Refunds are entirely at the discretion of the seller operating the Stripe account.

Disputes and Malicious Sellers

If you encounter a seller who is refusing a valid refund or selling fraudulent items, please contact our support team at support@rabyte.com. While we cannot issue the refund ourselves, we will investigate the seller and remove their listings from our platform to protect the community.
