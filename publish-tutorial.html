How to Publish a Product

A quick 3-step tutorial to get your digital product listed on Rabyte-Tech.

Step 1: Host your Product & Checkout

Rabyte-Tech does not host your files. First, upload your digital product and set up a checkout page on a platform like Gumroad, Lemon Squeezy, or Stripe Payment Links.

Step 2: Submit to Rabyte-Tech

Log in to your Seller Dashboard and click the "+ Submit Product" button. Fill in the form with:

  • A high-quality cover image (16:9 ratio recommended).
  • A detailed description of what the buyer will get.
  • The direct URL to your external checkout page from Step 1.

Step 3: Approval & Sales

Our moderation team will review your submission within 24-48 hours. Once approved, your product will be live on our marketplace, and 100% of the traffic and revenue will flow directly to your checkout link.
