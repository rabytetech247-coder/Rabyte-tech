Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

How is Rabyte-Tech completely free?

Unlike marketplaces that take a 20-30% cut of your sales, Rabyte-Tech operates purely as an aggregator. We drive SEO and organic traffic to our site, and then route users directly to your own checkout links (like Gumroad or Stripe). You keep 100% of your earnings.

I bought a product and need support. Who do I contact?

Please contact the creator of the product directly. Because payments are processed on the creator's platform (e.g., Lemon Squeezy, Stripe), we do not have access to your payment information, nor can we provide technical support for third-party templates.

How do I list my SaaS template or UI kit?

Simply create a free account, click "Submit Product", and fill out the details including your direct checkout link. Once our moderation team approves it, your product will be live and indexed on our platform.

Can I get a refund if the product is broken?

Refunds are handled entirely by the seller and their payment processor. You must request a refund through your original email receipt. If a seller is acting maliciously and denying valid refunds, please contact our support team so we can remove them from the directory.

Does Rabyte-Tech host my files?

No. You host your files on your own preferred platform (Gumroad, Payhip, Dropbox). We only host the listing, the descriptions, and the images to help buyers discover you.
