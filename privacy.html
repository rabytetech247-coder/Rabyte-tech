Privacy Policy

How we handle and protect your data.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account on Rabyte-Tech, we collect minimal personal information to provide you with a functional experience. This includes your email address, chosen username, and encrypted password hash. If you submit a product, we collect the public URLs associated with your product.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to authenticate your account, maintain your product listings, and send you critical platform updates (like password resets). We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

3. Cookies and Tracking

We use essential HTTP-only cookies to keep you logged in securely. We do not use invasive third-party tracking pixels (like Facebook Pixel) to track your behavior across the web.

4. Third-Party Links

Our marketplace contains links to external checkout pages (e.g., Stripe, Lemon Squeezy). Once you leave Rabyte-Tech, our privacy policy no longer applies. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any external sites you visit.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures, including Edge caching, D1 database encryption, and secure HTTPS connections, to protect your data from unauthorized access.
